Delete your ParkIt account

In the app (immediate)

Profile → Settings → Delete account → confirm. The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email:

parkit.app.il@gmail.com

Subject "Delete account", with the phone number or email you sign in with. We confirm the request came from the account owner and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Everything: account, phone, email, name, car details, points, reports, photos and feedback. No copy is kept.
